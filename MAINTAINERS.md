## How decisions are made

Day to day: maintainers merge focused pull requests that come with
verification. Protocol changes follow the proposal process in
[docs/governance.md](docs/governance.md). Releases ship when the gate written
down for them is met.

Decisions are made in public: an issue, a Discussion or a proposal thread.
If you cannot find the reasoning for a decision, ask; we would rather write
it down than have it be folklore.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are invited by the lead after sustained, reviewable
contributions in an area: a run of merged PRs, a world people use, a protocol
proposal carried through to a bundle release. There is no application form.
If you want to get there, pick an area and keep showing up.

## Continuity

See [docs/governance.md](docs/governance.md#if-the-lead-maintainer-stops).
The short version: everything is Apache-2.0, the last baseline stays valid
forever, and a successor or a fork is the plan, in that order.
